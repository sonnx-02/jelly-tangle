Level baker

For each level this searches generator attempts until one is solvable and its random-bot fail rate sits in the level's band (easy 0–25%, normal 25–55%, hard 55–85%, super 85–95%, or the level's own target). The exact level is then stored as data, re-loaded, and solved again to prove the stored copy is the level that was tested. Hand-made levels (designed: true in js/core.js: the designers' sheet, brought in by tools/import_levels.py, and level 2, the rainbow) are kept as they are, after the same check. Bake writes js/level-data.js (needs python tools/dev_server.py).
